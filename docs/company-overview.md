# Company overview

PMRASYSTEM LLC is a U.S. technology company developing advanced infrastructure and analytical systems for electronic markets. PMRA Systems is its principal technology initiative.

Registered / Mailing Address: 1021 E Lincolnway, 10936, Cheyenne, WY 82001, United States.

Primary work: electronic market infrastructure and analytics. PMRASYSTEM LLC also plans to develop a separate technology-enabled commerce business line (Commerce Operations — Planned).

PMRA Systems provides technology, research and analytical infrastructure and does not provide brokerage, custody or personalized investment advisory services.
