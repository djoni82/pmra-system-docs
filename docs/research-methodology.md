# Research methodology

Research Validation Framework:

1. **Hypothesis** — Research hypotheses are documented before evaluation.
2. **Data qualification** — Dataset integrity, completeness and timing semantics are validated before analysis.
3. **Causal measurement** — Market effects are separated from transport and execution effects.
4. **Out-of-sample validation** — Results are evaluated on data outside the fitting window.
5. **Execution modeling** — Execution models account for queue position, fees and cancellation behavior.
6. **Cost attribution** — Costs and economic effects are attributed by component.
7. **Reproducibility** — Inputs, methods and classification criteria are reproducible.

Simulation, shadow observation and runtime records are treated as distinct evidence classes.

Proprietary alpha and candidate-search logic remain private.
